TutoMart Pricing

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

🛡️ Our Core Commitment: We strictly respect your privacy. TutoMart Pricing does not sell, rent, or trade your personal data to third parties, advertisers, or data brokers. All collected data is strictly utilized to provide automated price history, coupon discovery, and shopping recommendations.

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy applies to the TutoMart Pricing Chrome Extension and its associated web services (collectively referred to as "TutoMart", "we", "us", or "our"). Our primary mission is to assist online shoppers in making informed purchasing decisions by tracking product prices, testing discount coupons, and analyzing product reviews.

2. Single Purpose & Data Minimization

In accordance with Google Chrome Web Store policies, TutoMart Pricing has a single, narrow purpose: to serve as a smart shopping companion for product price tracking and savings. We collect and process only the minimum amount of data required to deliver these functionalities.

3. Information We Collect and Why

We only collect data strictly essential for the extension's core features:

A. Authentication & Account Information

  • Data Collected: Email address, user identifier, and session tokens.
  • Purpose: To authenticate your TutoMart account, maintain your logged-in session, and synchronize your saved price alerts across devices.

B. E-Commerce Web History & Product URLs

  • Data Collected: The URL and domain of supported e-commerce product pages you actively browse (e.g., product page URL, product ASIN/SKU).
  • Purpose: To query our pricing database, fetch historical price trends (30/90-day charts), verify available promo codes, and display review summaries for that item.
  • Important: We do not monitor, track, or record your general browsing history, search queries, or activity on non-shopping websites.

C. Website Content & Price Information

  • Data Collected: Current product price, product name, category, and review text from supported shopping pages.
  • Purpose: To compute whether the current item is at a historical low, evaluate the "Buy or Wait" recommendation signal, and extract AI review sentiment highlights.

4. Browser Permissions & Justifications

The Chrome Extension requests specific permissions strictly required for its operation:

Permission Purpose & Justification
storage Saves your local extension preferences, cached auth state, and target price alert thresholds locally on your browser.
alarms Schedules background synchronization to check price alert thresholds and refresh expired coupon codes at periodic intervals.
notifications Delivers browser notifications when a tracked product drops below your set price threshold or when verified coupons are found.
activeTab Enables the extension to read the current shopping page URL and DOM only when you invoke the extension interface.
tabs Monitors tab activation to dynamically update the extension toolbar icon (active on supported shopping stores, disabled elsewhere).
cookies Reads the session cookies from TutoMart (tutomart.com) to synchronize login state between our web application and the extension.
host_permissions Allows the extension content script to extract product pricing and coupon fields across supported online shopping platforms.

5. Limited Use & Non-Sale of Data Certification

TutoMart complies with Google Chrome Web Store User Data Policies:

  • No Sale of Data: We do not sell, rent, or monetize user data or personal information under any circumstances.
  • Limited Use: We do not use or transfer user data for purposes unrelated to the extension's core shopping and price-tracking functionality.
  • No Credit Scoring or Lending: We never use or transfer user data to evaluate creditworthiness or for financial lending purposes.
  • No Remote Code: The extension does not load or execute external remote JavaScript code. All code is bundled within the extension archive.

6. Data Security and Storage

All data transmitted between the Chrome extension and our backend API is encrypted using industry-standard Transport Layer Security (TLS/HTTPS). Local settings and tokens are stored securely within Chrome's sandboxed local storage (chrome.storage.local).

7. User Control, Data Retention & Deletion

You maintain full control over your data at all times:

  • Extension Removal: You can uninstall the TutoMart Pricing extension at any time via chrome://extensions, which immediately deletes all locally stored data.
  • Account Deletion: You can delete your account and associated price alert records by visiting your user dashboard on the TutoMart website or by contacting support.

8. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may occasionally update this Privacy Policy to reflect improvements. Any revisions will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
